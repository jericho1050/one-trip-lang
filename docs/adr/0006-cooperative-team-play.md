# Cooperative team play and hidden bot reveal

Status: accepted by explicit user clarification, October 21, 2026. Supersedes earlier competitive hit-award wording.

## Decision

All four seats are teammates, including the hidden bots. Everyone helps squash the cockroach. Finalized squashes contribute to one shared team score. At the end, human players guess which teammates were bots; personal spotting results remain separate. No sabotage, elimination or bot-specific victory condition.

Slipper Court is a separate evidence feature that players may open after a miss. It tests deliberate recorded delay and does not affect team points or determine bot identity. It remains part of the planned engineering demonstration; opening Court is not required to finish the basic game loop.

## Consequences

Remove individual hit leaderboards and winning-seat language. Keep throw attribution only for replay and evidence. Bots should help hit the target, not maximize individual score. No additional communication, coordinated-planning or sabotage system is introduced by this clarification.
